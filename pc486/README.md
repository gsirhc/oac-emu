# 486 DX2-66 Gaming PC

Not a single factory model. A 1993-94 gamer's 486, the kind of box you'd have built or bought then.

## Specs

- CPU: Intel 80486DX2-66. 33 MHz bus, clock doubled, FPU on the die.
- RAM: 32 MB
- Modes: real mode, 16-bit and 32-bit protected mode, paging, virtual-8086
- Video: VGA and EGA, plus VESA BIOS Extensions. 1 MB of video RAM, mode 13h, 256 colors up to 1024x768.
- Sound: Sound Blaster 16 (DSP 4.05), 16-bit stereo DMA, OPL3, MPU-401 UART MIDI, and the PC speaker
- Storage: 256 MB WD Caviar AC2250, one 3.5 inch floppy (1.44 MB and 720 KB, NEC 765), and a 2x ATAPI CD-ROM with CD audio
- Input: keyboard and a 3-button PS/2 mouse
- Firmware: Bochs legacy BIOS and the LGPL VGA BIOS, with VESA
- OS: FreeDOS 1.3. Compatible with PC-DOS and MS-DOS 6.x software.

No game port, no SB-MIDI at ports 30h/31h, and no serial or parallel ports.
