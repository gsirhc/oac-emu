# Altair 8800

The MITS Altair 8800, the 1975 Intel 8080 machine. Front panel, paper tape, cassette, and an 8-inch floppy. Presets cover 4K BASIC, 8K BASIC, and CP/M.

## Specs

- CPU: Intel 8080 at 2 MHz
- RAM: 4K, 32K, or 64K, depending on the preset
- Front panel: address and data lights, and the real switches
- Serial: MITS 88-2SIO, ports `0x10`–`0x13`
- Terminals: Teletype ASR-33, Glass TTY, DEC VT52, Lear Siegler ADM-3A, DEC VT100
- Paper tape: MITS reader, 10 characters a second on the ASR-33
- Cassette: MITS 88-ACR
- Floppy: MITS 88-DCDD, 8-inch, enough to boot CP/M
- Software: MITS 4K BASIC, 8K BASIC, Star Trek on cassette, and a CP/M 2.2 disk

The 1975 presets use the 88-2SIO, which is a 1976 board. That's a labelled departure.
