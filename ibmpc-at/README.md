# IBM PC/AT

An IBM 5170, the 8 MHz 80286.

## Specs

- CPU: Intel 80286 at 8 MHz. Real mode and 286 protected mode.
- RAM: 512 KB or 640 KB conventional, plus up to 4 MB extended
- Video: EGA
- Sound: PC speaker, and drive noise
- Storage: 30 MB hard disk on a WD1003. 5.25 inch floppy A: is 1.2 MB, B: is 360 KB (NEC 765).
- Input: keyboard. Serial mouse on COM1 (opt-in, needs a DOS driver).
- Printer: LPT1 print capture
- Firmware: Bochs legacy BIOS, and an open EGA BIOS
- OS: FreeDOS 1.3. Compatible with PC-DOS and MS-DOS 3.x software.

No sound card, no game port, and no 386 software. That means no EMM386 and no Windows enhanced mode.
