System specs

      

        
CPU80286 (8 MHz)

        
RAM512 or 640 KB conventional, plus up to 4 MB extended (see Memory)

        
ModesReal and protected mode (80286)

        
GraphicsEGA

        
SoundPC speaker

        
Storage30 MB HDD (WD1003), 5.25″ floppy A: 1.2MB / B: 360KB (NEC 765)

        
InputKeyboard; serial mouse on COM1 (opt-in, needs a DOS driver such as CuteMouse)

        
PrinterLPT1 capture; a dialog offers download when a job finishes

        
BIOSBochs legacy BIOS and an open EGA BIOS. IBM's original ROMs are still under copyright.

        
OSShips with FreeDOS 1.3, compatible with PC-DOS / MS-DOS 3.x

        
Not includedSound card, game port, 386 software (EMM386, Windows enhanced mode)
